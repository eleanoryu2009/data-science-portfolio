# Data Science & Business Analytics Portfolio
Welcome! This repository showcases my independent projects bridging "Python (Machine Learning)" and "R (Statistical & Economic Analysis)".
My academic interests lie at the intersection of Statistics, Economics, and Business Technology Management (BTM). I use quantitative methods to bridge the gap between macro-level economic trends and micro-level business strategy.
## Technical Stack
-Languages: Python, R
-Libraries: Pandas, Scikit-learn, LightGBM, tidyverse, ggplot2
-Tools: Jupyter Notebook, RStudio, Kaggle, Github
## Featured Projects
### 1. Retail Sales Forecasting (Python/Machine Learning)
-Methodology: Bulit a LightGBM regression model. Extracted business features like "promotions" and "paydays."
-Insights: Promotions heavily drive retail sales. Dynamic inventory management is recommended during promotional periods.
-code: [View code](./retail_sales_kaggle_project.ipynb)
### 2. Customer Churn Prediction (Python/Classification)
-Methodology: Logistic Regression with StandardScaler. Validated via 5-Fold Cross-Validation.
-Insight: Monthly charges are the top driver of churn. Recommended tiered loyalty programs.
-code: [View code](./customer_churn_prediction_and_business_insights.ipynb)
-Report: [Read Detailed Business Report](./Churn_Report.md)
### 3. A/B Testing & Bootstrapping (R/Statistical Inference)
-Methodology: Simulated A/B test; used 1000 Bootstrap resamples to calculate the 95% Confidence Interval.
-Insight: Confirmed the statistical significance of the new UI's higher conversion rate.
-Code & Chart: [View code](./ab_testing_analysis.R)|[View Chart](./ab_testing_plot.png)
### 4. E-commerce Discount Strategy (R/Business Analytics)
-Methodology: Feature engineering on discount tiers; visualized net revenue volatility using Boxplots.
-Insight: Deep discounts increase revenue volatility. Recommended targeted promotions to protect margins. 
-Code & Chart: [View code](./ecommerce_discount_analysis.R|[View Chart](./discount_analysis_plot.png)
-Report: [Read Detailed Business Report](./Ecommerce_Report.md)
### 5. Macroeconomic Trends (R/Economics)
-Methodology: Aggregated monthly data into annual trends; visualized Consumption vs. Unemployment.
-Insight: Unemployment shows cyclical fluctuations with delayed peaks during recessions (e.g. 2008 Crisis).
-Code & Chart: [View Code](./macroeconomic_trends_analysis.R)|[View Chart](./macroeconomic_chart.png)
